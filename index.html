Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding our Canadian IPTV infrastructure, billing, and performance

What is IPTV and how does it work in Canada? ▼

IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) transmits digital TV broadcasts via the internet rather than traditional coaxial cables or satellite dishes. When you select a channel or movie, data packets travel through secure, local Canadian internet exchange points straight to your streaming device.

Can I use my IPTV subscription on multiple devices at once? ▼

Yes! By selecting a multi-device plan (from 2 up to 5 devices), each family member can watch separate sports broadcasts, live movies, or local news on different screens simultaneously without drops in connection.

Is IPTV legal in Canada? ▼

Yes. IPTV technology is completely compliant in Canada when accessing content through authorized provider setups. We provide secure network access and dedicated technical support, ensuring viewers stay safely connected.

What internet speed do I need for smooth streaming? ▼

A stable download connection of 15 Mbps is recommended for smooth HD streaming, and 25-30 Mbps is ideal for 4K Ultra HD and 60FPS live sports.

How do I activate my IPTV subscription on my device? ▼

Once your Interac e-Transfer is verified, our automated dispatcher sends an activation email containing your M3U playlist URL, Xtream Codes credentials (Username, Password, Server URL), and quick setup instructions for your chosen app.
